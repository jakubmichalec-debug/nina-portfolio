About

About me

I’m a creative person with a passion for marketing, art and business, and I enjoy projects that bring all three together.

My background combines both sides. I graduated from IWK Art School in Slovakia, where I trained in painting, drawing, graphic design and fashion design, and from a Business Academy with a focus on entrepreneurship, economics, marketing and accounting.

Today I study Multimedia & Creative Technologies at KdG in Antwerp, working across design, content and digital media.

Alongside my studies I run Niana, my own brand of handmade crochet, leather and denim bags. I do everything myself, from making the bags to the brand identity, photography, reels and website, and I’m building an audience ahead of the launch.

I’m now looking for a three-month internship where I can contribute to a creative team and keep growing professionally.

Pinball machine
University project · FabLab, team of three

A pinball machine, built from scratch.

This was a university project we did as a team of three. Each of us brought a different skill, mechanical, electronic and visual work, and we combined them into one working pinball machine built in KdG's FabLab. It took about six months from the first idea to a machine you can actually play.

3D printingVisual designLayout
Chapter 1

The idea

We chose to make a pinball machine because it mixes everything: moving parts, electronics and a lot of design. None of us had built one before, so we started with research and small tests.

My part was the creative work: 3D printing, the visuals, the design and the layout of the playfield.

The finished machine
Chapter 2

Gallery

The finished machine, the visual concept and the artwork, up close and from every side. I built a psychedelic, surreal mood from a Pinterest moodboard and a five color palette, and painted every visible surface by hand.

I also designed the playfield layout, where the targets, ramps, barriers and flippers sit, and tested it until the ball moved the way we wanted.

University project · Brand & UI design

Coachella, where my graphic design started.

This was a university project at KdG. We could choose any brand to redesign, and I picked Coachella. I rebranded it from the ground up with a new logo and mood, an app concept, and the social and print materials a festival like this needs. It was the start of my graphic design work, and the project that made me want to keep going in that direction.

Project · Motion graphics, After Effects

Yuzu Fest, a festival I invented.

For a motion graphics assignment I made up my own festival. I came up with the name, drew the logo and picked the colors, then animated the on-screen graphics in After Effects, the kind you see when an artist is introduced on a festival broadcast.

Motion graphicsAfter EffectsLogoBranding
Chapter 1

Brand & moodboard

Yuzu Fest had to feel loud, playful and a bit trippy. The moodboard is full of liquid color, neon light and blurred crowds, with a few fake sponsors and a first sketch of the logo as a tear.

The logo is a drop and a chunky, curvy wordmark in dark blue, magenta and yellow, so it stands out on any screen.

Moodboard
The logo
Chapter 2

In motion

The final part is the animation, all built in After Effects. First the logo drops in and reveals itself, then come the name plates that introduce each artist on the lineup. Every plate carries the Yuzu colors, fonts and logo, so the whole festival feels like one brand.
